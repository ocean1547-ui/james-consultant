My Library — Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026 · Applies to My Library and My Library Free (Android)

How the app works

My Library is a personal reading app. You can add public-domain books from connected public libraries and read them in English and Korean side by side, and you can turn a video of your own book's pages into a readable book in your private library. Text-to-speech uses your device's built-in voice.

Account and data we store

The app requires an account so that your library belongs only to you. You can sign up with an email address or with your Google account. We store your email address, your library (books you added, reading positions, and books converted from your videos), and your monthly conversion usage. Each account's library is kept separate and is never visible to other users.

Your book videos

When you use "Make my book", the video you choose is uploaded to our server, converted into text, and the original video is deleted after conversion. The converted book is stored only in your own account's library and is not shared with other users.

Public library books

Books offered through the app's library catalogue are public-domain works. When a catalogue book is converted (for example, translated), the converted copy is kept on our server so other readers can open it without converting it again. This applies only to public-domain catalogue books, never to books made from your own videos.

Advertising (free version)

My Library Free displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID and ad interaction data, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive or store this data ourselves. The subscription version contains no advertising.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the service providers needed to run the app (such as sign-in and AI conversion providers), and only to the extent needed for the feature you used.

Children

This app is a general reading tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
