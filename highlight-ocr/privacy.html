Highlight OCR — Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026 · Applies to Highlight OCR and Highlight OCR Free (Android)

How the app works

Highlight OCR extracts text from photos of highlighted passages. All optical character recognition (OCR) is performed entirely on your device using an embedded OCR engine. Your photos and the text extracted from them are never uploaded to our servers.

Data we collect

Highlight OCR (paid version): We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app works fully offline and requires no account.

Highlight OCR Free: The OCR features work the same way — your photos and text stay on your device. The free version displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID and ad interaction data, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive or store this data ourselves.

Permissions

  • Camera — used only to capture the page you want to scan, at the moment you take the photo.
  • Photos / storage — used only when you choose an existing image from your device for text extraction.

Data sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data. In the free version, advertising data handling is performed by Google AdMob under Google's own policies, as described above.

Children

This app is a general productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
